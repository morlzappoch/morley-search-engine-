## Traffic

The public site does not expose private Netlify analytics. Actual visitor/pageview counts must be read from the associated **Netlify Project Analytics** account.

Recommended metrics to preserve:

- Pageviews
- Unique visitors
- Top pages
- Referrers / traffic sources
- Geographic distribution
- Bandwidth
- Date/time range

Do not record credentials, access tokens, private analytics exports, or other sensitive account information in the public repository.
